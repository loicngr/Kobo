# Community conduct

Be respectful, describe technical disagreements with evidence, and welcome users
with different tools, experience and working habits. Harassment, threats,
discriminatory remarks and disclosure of another person's private information
are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

For private conduct reports, contact the maintainer at the email listed in
`package.json` (`loicnogier@ik.me`). Include links and relevant context, avoiding
unnecessary personal data. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private channel
described in `SECURITY.md`.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close a thread, or restrict participation
to protect contributors and users. Decisions should be proportionate to the
behavior and context. Reports are handled privately as far as practical; no
response-time commitment is offered.
